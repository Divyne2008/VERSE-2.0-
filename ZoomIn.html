After the Rain

Mara Santos

The city smelled like wet concrete
and forgotten promises.
I walked the long way home
counting puddles, each one
holding a small, borrowed sky.

Somewhere a window lit up.
Somewhere a stranger laughed
at something I would never know.

By the time I reached my door
the streetlights had learned my name,
and the rain, tired of asking,
finally let me be.
